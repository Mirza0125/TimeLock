## Flagged Assumptions (from the spec-less edge probe, FND-01)

- **"Clean checkout" scope.** It means a fresh `git clone` of HEAD with no node_modules/ and no android/, built on this dev machine, reusing its persistent ~/.gradle, pnpm store, nvm Node, JDK and SDK. A cold-cache build on a different machine (or CI) is not exercised in Phase 1. The spec's CI pipeline (§21) is where that gets proven later.
